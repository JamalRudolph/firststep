When you need a voice, not a script

Some moments need a person on the other end.

FirstStep gives you the steps and the words. Sometimes that's not what you need. When you need to talk to a real person — these helplines are free, confidential, and answer 24/7. You do not have to be in crisis to call.

988
Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Mental health, emotional distress, substance use.
Call or text 988
Crisis Text Line
Any moment of distress. Text-based, anonymous.
Text HOME to 741741
Domestic Violence Hotline
Abuse, control, unsafe partner or family member.
Call 1-800-799-7233
RAINN
Sexual assault survivor support. Free, confidential.
Call 1-800-656-4673
