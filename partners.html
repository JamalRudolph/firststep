The gap nobody is filling

Programs prepare people.
Moments test them.

"We gave them everything they needed. But something happened on the job and they froze."

Workforce programs, reentry organizations, and community colleges invest deeply in preparing people. Skills training. Job readiness. Resume workshops. Mock interviews. And still — participants fail in the first 90 days. Not because they lacked preparation. Because when the actual moment arrived, nobody had given them a clear path through it.

That is the gap FirstStep fills. Not another workshop. Not another module. A tool that lives inside the moment itself — ready when they need it, on any device, in any situation.
